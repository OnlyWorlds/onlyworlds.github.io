OnlyWorlds is free to use and extend.

## Questions and bugs

- **A bug, or a question about a package or tool**: open an issue on its repository: the [TypeScript SDK](https://github.com/OnlyWorlds/sdk/issues), the [Python package](https://github.com/OnlyWorlds/python-sdk/issues), the [Unity SDK](https://github.com/OnlyWorlds/unity-sdk/issues), [Atlas](https://github.com/OnlyWorlds/atlas/issues), [Lantern](https://github.com/OnlyWorlds/lantern/issues), the [Obsidian plugin](https://github.com/OnlyWorlds/obsidian-plugin/issues), the [toolkit](https://github.com/OnlyWorlds/toolkit/issues), or [these docs](https://github.com/OnlyWorlds/onlyworlds.github.io/issues). A problem with onlyworlds.com or its API goes to [Feedback](https://www.onlyworlds.com/feedback). These repositories are maintained by AI members of the OnlyWorlds crew, and the founder answers for them.
- **A vulnerability**: report it privately, never in an issue. The [TypeScript SDK](https://github.com/OnlyWorlds/sdk/security/advisories/new), the [Python package](https://github.com/OnlyWorlds/python-sdk/security/advisories/new), the [Unity SDK](https://github.com/OnlyWorlds/unity-sdk/security/advisories/new), [these docs](https://github.com/OnlyWorlds/onlyworlds.github.io/security/advisories/new) and the [tools list](https://github.com/OnlyWorlds/tools/security/advisories/new) take reports through GitHub's private reporting, and each `SECURITY.md` says what counts; a report about anything else can go through any of them and is passed to its maintainer. These docs publish the route at [/.well-known/security.txt](https://onlyworlds.github.io/.well-known/security.txt).

- **[Council](/docs/tools/council).** Delegate your characters to shape the OnlyWorlds standard ([council.onlyworlds.com](https://council.onlyworlds.com)).
