The primary OnlyWorlds workspace. The folder is the world: your world lives as plain JSON files on your own machine, and Atlas reads and writes them there. Nothing is locked away.

**URL:** [https://atlas.onlyworlds.com](https://atlas.onlyworlds.com)
**Type:** Local-first Web Application (Chromium desktop)
**What's new:** [changelog](https://atlas.onlyworlds.com/changelog)

---

## Features

- **Build & Browse** - All 22 element types in a schema-driven editor; link fields are pickers, and every element lists what links to it
- **Write** - Books, chapters and scenes (the tier names are yours to set); names in your prose become links with a click, the find bar can link every match at once, and the words stay yours
- **Web** - Any element's connections as a small graph, including everything that links to it
- **Map** - Your own map images, nested maps, pins for any element, and zones drawn as areas or lines; colour zones by kind (rivers blue, roads dashed) and the style is saved with the world
- **Chart** - Place elements on a time axis by their dates, on your world's own calendar; save charts and add them to published pages
- **Knowledge** - Track who knows what, per character or circle, and see the world as one of them knows it
- **Calendars** - Custom calendars as a display layer over world time
- **Local-first** - Worlds are folders of plain JSON files you own; works offline
- **Sync** - Link a world to onlyworlds.com to push and pull; the folder stays the source of truth
- **Import / Export** - Import and export the same world file onlyworlds.com exports; a world folder written by another tool opens in place
- **Sharing** - Publish read-only pages of your world you can send to anyone (needs an account; per-account page quota)
- **Light and dark** - Both themes across the whole app
- **No signup to start** - A guided tour builds a small demo world with you. Folder mode needs a desktop Chromium browser (Chrome, Edge or Brave); recent Firefox and Safari can take the tour
